About

I am Shubha Tanaya, a first-year student pursuing my Master of Science in Robotics Systems Development (MRSD) at Carnegie Mellon University . I recently transitioned from a Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar.

My current work includes a range of autonomous systems projects at CMU, undertaken alongside my first-semester coursework, reflecting my drive to push myself beyond the classroom. I am particularly interested in full-stack autonomous systems, with a focus on integration and perception, as I enjoy working across the entire pipeline from sensing to planning to control.

At DRIVE Lab, I contributed to F1Tenth Autonomous Racing, optimizing a ROS2 stack for high-speed performance on NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX, integrating LiDAR-based SLAM with Particle Filters for precise localization, and developing a dual-tier navigation system for obstacle avoidance and trajectory tracking. At AIR Lab, I implemented whole-body inverse kinematics for UAV–manipulator systems using CasADi, DH forward kinematics, and SO(3) quaternion control. I also contributed to an industry collaboration with BMW, identifying opportunities for AI-powered robotic interventions in product development and engineering workflows.

I am passionate about Collaborative Autonomous Systems in high-stakes environments such as search and rescue, disaster relief, and environmental monitoring, where safety and efficiency are critical. Set to graduate in Spring 2027, I am eager to contribute to the robotics industry by leading projects and teams that deploy autonomous systems in impactful scenarios, pushing the boundaries of robotics while driving meaningful societal change.

Seeking a role in robotics and autonomous systems, specializing in perception and full-stack integration, to foster professional growth and thrive in an innovative environment.
